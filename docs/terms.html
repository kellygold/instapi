1. What InstaPi Is

InstaPi is free, open-source software (MIT License) that turns a Raspberry Pi into a digital picture frame for locally stored photos. It runs on your own hardware and displays locally stored photos, including while offline. There is no hosted InstaPi service or subscription. Optional Google Photos imports and remote frame sharing require a network connection.

3. Google Photos Connection

Google Photos is an optional import source. Google sign-in authorizes the InstaPi installation running on your own hardware; it does not create an InstaPi account or connect to an InstaPi-hosted service. The installation must have a Google OAuth registration and callback configured for it. By connecting Google Photos:

  • You authorize InstaPi (running on your own hardware) to access photos you explicitly select via Google's Photo Picker.
  • The Google Photos connection is optional and depends on Google’s service and the registration configured for your installation.
  • You can configure your own Google OAuth registration. Setup instructions are in the project README.
